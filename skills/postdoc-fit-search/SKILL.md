# Postdoc fit search

Read the configured private profile or the blank [candidate template](../../docs/CANDIDATE_PROFILE.md), then [search workflow](../../docs/SEARCH_WORKFLOW.md). User requirements and reviewed evidence control the scope. Do not assume an H-1B preference, acceptable OPT bridge, enrollment status, start date, or degree completion date from this skill.

Keep two discovery paths separate:

- **Recruitment:** Official ATS, institution, department, and laboratory pages. Read the complete requirements, dates, funding language, application route, and appointment terms. Aggregators are discovery sources.
- **Funding:** Official award records, such as NIH RePORTER and NSF Award Search, identify research-aligned PIs. Funding alone creates a contact lead, not an open vacancy.

The sibling [LinkedIn skill](../linkedin-hiring-post-search/SKILL.md) can supplement discovery with postdoc-specific public terms. Do not treat an individual post as an official vacancy or a request to send a message.

Save direct URLs, source dates, actual checked dates, access method, short evidence, and unresolved conflicts. API success can support API fields when a webpage fails; it does not prove the webpage was read. Zero search results or access failure do not prove absence of funding, vacancies, or sponsorship.

Assess five axes independently. A combined score must not cancel a hard conflict or pretend to estimate acceptance probability.

| Axis | Distinctions to preserve |
|---|---|
| Recruitment | Confirmed remaining opening; visible official advertisement; evergreen or undated recruitment; funding lead only; closed or filled; unknown. Visible advertising is not confirmation of an unfilled slot. |
| Funding | Written support for the candidate's appointment; advertisement states support; related award only; historical or unknown; explicitly unfunded or requires own funding. Record initial term and renewal conditions separately. |
| Research and requirements | Required, preferred, or unclear; candidate evidence; directly met, transferable, no evidence, or known mismatch. Missing evidence is not confirmed absence. Do not substitute coauthorship for first authorship or general ML for clinical expertise. |
| Sponsorship | Institution policy and this appointment's actual path, responsible department, approval conditions, and timing. Historical filings and international eligibility do not establish a commitment. |
| Mentoring and workload | Concrete hours, evening/weekend expectations, publication or renewal targets, teaching, supervision, data collection, administration, meeting cadence, resources, and career support. Unknown stays unknown. |

For funding, verify PI role, institution, grant mechanism, full and core award identifiers, fiscal year, budget and project periods, and amount basis. Do not count annual continuations, parent/subprojects, supplements, subawards, or multiple PIs as independent full awards. An annual award is not the remaining spendable balance; an extension is not automatically new funding. Related awards cannot establish how many people a PI can hire. Validate grant-specific trainee or fellowship restrictions from current official terms rather than inferring eligibility from a salary scale.

Use [sponsor-check](../sponsor-check/SKILL.md), reusing evidence only for the same legal employer and covered appointment type. Keep **institution policy** separate from **role path**. Identify employee payroll versus fellowship or stipend, formal appointment title/code, cap-exempt basis, wage conditions, initiating department, international-office approval, and any current state or institutional restriction. Do not infer an exemption from an institution's name or apply one employer's policy to a related hospital or foundation.

Keep internal initiation, petition filing, intended status effective date, and actual approval separate. "Start the process on joining" does not mean status is effective on the first day. An OPT bridge requires a specific allowed appointment, start trigger, responsible unit, intended transition, and unresolved conditions. An unspecified future possibility remains unknown. Do not apply a cap-subject annual registration workflow to a cap-exempt role without evidence. A different status does not automatically satisfy the configured preference.

Rank after hard gates, using direct research fit, vacancy and funding evidence, an executable appointment path, and working conditions. Do not use PI age, ethnicity, nationality, fame, institution ranking, or lab size as proxies for ease of admission or low pressure. Separate apply/verify priorities, conditional leads, and insufficient-evidence items. Keep funding-only leads separate and deduplicate by PI, institution, and confirmed role/req.

Provide a compact comparison with PI/institution/legal employer, role, research evidence and gaps, recruitment, funding term, institution policy, role sponsorship path, workload evidence, dates/URLs, and the smallest useful next action. Ask only questions needed to resolve the next decision. Work-culture claims require concrete expectations or direct accounts; contacting current or former members requires user authorization.

This skill permits local research and drafts within the task scope. It does not authorize outreach, applications, uploads, publication, or new monitors. Preserve existing decisions and actual application records. Do not export a private candidate profile or copy personal examples from a source installation.
