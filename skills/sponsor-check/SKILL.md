# Sponsorship assessment

Read the configured private profile or the blank [candidate template](../../docs/CANDIDATE_PROFILE.md), then the evidence conventions in [search workflow](../../docs/SEARCH_WORKFLOW.md). This is a research and decision framework. It does not establish the candidate's authorization or an employer's commitment.

## Reuse before new research

Look for reviewed employer evidence, including `工具/healthcare岗位抓取/ref/employer_visa.json` if configured locally. Match the legal entity, original source, actual checked date, and data coverage period. Preserve those dates and label reuse. A new role, session, model, or older date alone does not require a full repeated investigation.

Check this role's complete JD and application restrictions, actual employer, degree relevance, work location, pay, SOC, and wage basis. Refresh affected facts when there is a new entity, policy change, contradictory evidence, decision-relevant new disclosure data, evidence of expiry, or an offer needing actual arrangements confirmed. Do not inherit a parent's or affiliate's evidence automatically. Unknowns remain unknown. Validate global rules once per research run where appropriate, rather than once per role.

## Evidence and entity chain

Map the recruiting brand, offer entity, payroll entity, E-Verify entity, I-983 signer, and H-1B petitioner. Record unconfirmed links. For agency roles, evaluate the actual hiring/payroll arrangement. A brand-only search cannot establish a legal entity's absence from a database.

For each uncertain item, separate:

1. Query result: exact queried name, source, method, coverage period, and result or failure.
2. Established fact: what that evidence proves and what remains unknown.
3. Decision assumption: what the candidate can safely rely on pending confirmation.
4. Resolution: the specific evidence or written confirmation needed to change the assessment.

An empty result does not prove no experience, inability, or unwillingness. Check aliases and source coverage. Validate the same query method using a known positive control before interpreting a zero. Do not repeatedly rerun unchanged unknowns.

For a first employer investigation, read the actual company careers/policy source and complete ATS or formal recruiting page. A blocked page, JavaScript shell, snippet, or repost must be labeled as such. Reuse company evidence for the same entity; obtain role-specific current evidence. Explicit, authoritative negative policy applies to the stated path unless superseded or an applicable exception is confirmed. Positive language establishes stated willingness, not operational capacity or a promise for this candidate. Personal employee comments and third-party summaries do not establish employer policy.

Use current official sources for decision-relevant rules and record the checked date:

- [USCIS students and employment](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/students-and-exchange-visitors/students-and-employment), [USCIS STEM OPT](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/students-and-exchange-visitors/optional-practical-training-extension-for-stem-students-stem-opt), and [Study in the States](https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/) for candidate and employer conditions.
- [E-Verify employer search](https://www.e-verify.gov/about-e-verify/e-verify-data/how-to-find-participating-employers) and its published coverage limits. A public-search miss leaves registration unknown; obtain entity-specific confirmation and required training-plan support.
- [DOL disclosure data](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/performance) for LCA and PERM records, [USCIS H-1B Employer Data Hub](https://www.uscis.gov/tools/reports-and-studies/h-1b-employer-data-hub) for petition history, and [DOL H-1B guidance](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/immigration/h1b) for applicable wage and employer obligations. Filings are historical activity, not this role's commitment or petition approval.
- [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/), [current regulations](https://www.ecfr.gov/), and responsible official agencies for effective rules, fees, selection procedures, court effects, and exemptions. Distinguish proposals from effective requirements. This package contains no current fee, selection-weight, or lottery-policy snapshot.

If an interactive source repeatedly fails, use an available official download or record the limitation. Do not bypass access controls or turn failed access into a negative finding.

## Role and wage gates

Assess actual duties against the candidate's confirmed degree. A title alone does not prove relevance. Separate required citizenship/status, clearance, export-control, and current/future sponsorship clauses. Apply each exact clause to confirmed candidate facts; do not treat all export-control wording as an identical citizenship rule. If applicability is unclear, retain the wording and unresolved question.

Record the exact form question before drafting an answer. Present authorization, future need, citizenship, and legal status are different questions. Do not insert an assumed "Yes" or "No", infer a valid EAD from a graduation plan, or assume the candidate's eligibility for a particular status.

For wage analysis, obtain the role's SOC, actual work area, salary basis, and applicable wage year from evidence. Use official OFLC/OEWS data when available; identify missing files rather than inventing a level. Keep an LCA's `PW_WAGE_LEVEL` separate from any level computed from an offered salary. A posted range is not an offer; a wage lower bound is not a reported salary. Calculate and label a range or scenarios only where inputs justify them. Recheck current selection rules before describing any consequence of a wage level. Do not emit cached ticket counts or probability claims.

## Four independent conclusions

Use 🟢 supported within stated conditions, 🟡 conditional/unknown, or 🔴 explicit conflict for each path. Green never means approval or guaranteed start. If a path is outside the candidate's intended plan, say so and retain its evidence status rather than inventing a need.

| Path | Explain separately |
|---|---|
| Initial OPT | Confirmed candidate authorization/timing, degree relevance, and applicable work conditions; unknown authorization stays conditional. |
| STEM OPT | Candidate eligibility and employer entity, E-Verify, training-plan, supervision, and employment conditions under current rules. A public-search miss alone is not red. |
| H-1B | Stated willingness, filing experience, actual role commitment, cap-subject or exempt basis, pay, responsible unit, approvals, timing, and current applicable rules. |
| PERM | Employer policy and historical activity versus a candidate-specific commitment. Employer PERM support does not describe every possible immigration route. |

Include the entity chain, sources/dates/coverage, exact policy language, degree-related duties, wage analysis or missing inputs, current-policy validation status, four tiers, and the next useful action. State what can support an application decision and what needs written confirmation before an offer decision. Keep employer promises, internal approvals, filings, and government approvals separate.

For a young or financially uncertain employer, assess official funding/runway evidence and employment continuity without estimating unsupported survival probabilities. Clarify actual payroll, appointment terms, benefits, termination provisions, and support arrangements. Do not copy fixed grace periods or personal timeline statements into a plan without current validation.

Stage employer questions to the decision at hand. Draft concise English using confirmed candidate facts. An offer-stage checklist may cover entity roles, duties/location/pay, training support, intended filing cycle or exempt path, internal approvals, and legally required employer costs. Research does not authorize sending the questions, applying, uploading documents, or creating a monitor; honor specific direct authorization already given.
